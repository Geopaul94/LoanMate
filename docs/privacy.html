LoanMate: EMI & Loan Tracker

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 August 2026

LoanMate (“the app”) is developed by Geo Paulson (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

By default, LoanMate stores everything on your device only. There are no accounts, no ads, and no analytics. The only optional network feature is Google Drive backup, which you must turn on yourself and which only your app's private Drive folder can access.

1. Data you enter

Loan details you add — type, lender, principal, interest rate, tenure, EMI amount, payment history, and any notes — are stored in a private database on your own device. This data never leaves your phone unless you explicitly enable Google Drive backup (see below) or export a PDF/JSON file yourself.

2. Optional Google Drive backup

LoanMate can back up your loan data to your own Google Drive, if and only if you sign in and turn this on in Settings. This uses Google's most restricted Drive scope, drive.appdata (the “app data folder”):

  • Backups are invisible in the normal Drive UI — only LoanMate can read or write them.
  • We (the developers) cannot see, access, or download your Drive backups. Google does not grant app data folder access to anyone but the app itself, signed in as you.
  • The backup is the same JSON used for local export: loan details, payments, and achievements. No device identifiers, contacts, or other Drive files are touched.
  • The app keeps your last 5 backups on Drive and prunes older ones automatically.
  • You can disable this at any time in Settings, and revoke access entirely from your Google Account permissions page.

3. Local export & PDF

You can export your loan data as a JSON backup file or a PDF summary at any time. These files are created on your device and only shared if you explicitly choose to share them (e.g. via email or another app) — LoanMate never uploads them on its own.

4. App lock (biometric)

LoanMate optionally supports locking the app behind your device's fingerprint or face unlock. This is handled entirely by Android's own biometric system — the app never sees or stores your biometric data; it only receives a yes/no unlock result from the OS.

5. Permissions the app uses

  • Notifications / exact alarms — to remind you before an EMI is due.
  • Boot completed — to re-arm your reminders after the phone restarts.
  • Internet — used only when you actively sign in and use Google Drive backup; the app otherwise works fully offline.
  • Biometric / vibrate — for optional app-lock and reminder alerts.

These permissions are used solely for the functionality described above — never for tracking or advertising.

6. No ads, no analytics, no third-party trackers

LoanMate contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, and no crash-reporting services that transmit data off your device. The only third-party service the app talks to is Google Drive, and only when you turn on backup yourself.

7. Data deletion

Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all locally stored data. If you enabled Google Drive backup, you can delete those backups by disabling backup in Settings or by revoking LoanMate's access from your Google Account permissions page.

8. Children

LoanMate is a personal-finance tool intended for general audiences and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated and the change will be clearly reflected here, with the “last updated” date above revised accordingly.
